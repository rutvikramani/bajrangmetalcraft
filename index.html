About Us

Engineering & manufacturing built around quality, process control and your requirements

Bajrang Metalcraft is an engineering and manufacturing company based in Rajkot, Gujarat, focused on casting and component solutions for industrial applications.

Our primary casting focus is on SGI (Spheroidal Graphite Iron) and CI (Grey Cast Iron) components. Alongside casting, we offer fettling and finishing, precision machining and hot-dip galvanizing support.

Working from customer drawings, specifications and application requirements, we define the process route, coordinate critical stages and support inspection and delivery of processed components.

Manufacturing Process

From raw material to finished component

A defined seven-stage route, with control points matched to the component and the customer specification.

01

Material Selection

Controlled selection of charge materials and process inputs based on the required alloy, grade and component specification.

02

Melting & Metallurgy

Controlled melting with attention to chemistry, temperature and melt treatment to support consistent CI and SGI production.

03

Moulding & Core Making

Mould and core preparation suited to component geometry, internal features, casting soundness and required finish.

04

Pouring & Solidification

Controlled pouring and cooling, with treatment/inoculation as applicable to the selected material and route.

05

Shakeout & Fettling

Removal of sand, gates and risers, followed by grinding, cleaning and surface finishing as required.

06

Machining / Finishing

Precision machining and finishing to customer drawings, dimensions, tolerances and functional requirements.

07

Inspection & Dispatch

Final dimensional and visual checks, documentation, packing and delivery coordination.

Quality Assurance

Quality control built into the manufacturing process

Our quality approach rests on process discipline, inspection and continuous improvement. Control points are selected to match customer specifications and the agreed production route.

Chemical Analysis

Spectrometer-based verification of material chemistry against the required composition, where applicable.

Sand Testing

Green-sand process control covering key moulding parameters that influence mould quality and repeatability.

Metallography

Microstructural examination for metallurgical validation, including graphite morphology and matrix structure as applicable.

Mechanical Testing

Hardness and tensile testing as required by the applicable specification or customer requirement.

Dimensional Inspection

Precision measuring instruments and gauges for dimensional checks against approved drawings and tolerances.

Process Control

Heat, process and final inspection records supporting consistency, traceability and corrective action.

Quality is not a final step — it is built into the process.
